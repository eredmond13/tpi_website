Research

Research Hubs

Our cutting-edge thinking on the critical issues where technology meets national security and public policy.

01

Critical Infrastructure

From secure supply chains to next-generation data centers, the Critical Infrastructure Hub works across physical and digital systems to promote resilience and bolster national security.

The hub is home to the U.S. Semiconductor Research Hub, funded by a $3 million Department of Defense grant. That work centres on improving the resilience of the global interconnected network of semiconductor infrastructure and mitigating future threats, particularly cybersecurity threats. The hub identifies critical vulnerabilities in the semiconductor supply chain, develops targeted mitigation strategies, and informs effective investments to enhance resilience.

  • Semiconductor supply chains
  • Cybersecurity assessment
  • Data centre resilience
  • Academia, industry, and government collaboration

02

Drones and Robotics

Catalysed by funding from the NATO Science for Peace and Security Programme, the Drones and Robotics Hub focuses on the development and use of military drone technologies across air, land, and sea.

Current work provides NATO members and partner states with strategic insights on identifying and countering next-generation drone threats, especially lethal autonomous threats, across all domains.

  • Full Spectrum Drone Warfare
  • Counter-drone policy
  • Lethal autonomous systems
  • NATO partnership

04

Cryptocurrencies

Funded by the Human Rights Foundation and the Reynolds Foundation, the Cryptocurrencies Hub studies how citizens of authoritarian governments secure financial freedom in the face of suppressive financial controls, and investigates how Bitcoin and stablecoins are used around the world.

The goal of this research is to create a global adoption index, mapping cryptocurrency usage in order to advise policymakers, industry leaders, and communities on crafting effective strategies to secure financial freedom.

  • Financial freedom under authoritarianism
  • Bitcoin and stablecoin adoption
  • Global adoption index
  • Financial privacy
