Chris Earls' research focuses on using mathematics and physics for mechanistic interpretability of
complex, emergent behaviors exhibited by state-of-the-art AI systems. Such mechanistic understanding
is vital when deploying AI within high-regret contexts, and when forming trustworthy partnerships
between human and machine intelligence.

Earls is the J. Preston Levis Professor of Engineering and a faculty member in the Cornell Center for
Applied Mathematics.
