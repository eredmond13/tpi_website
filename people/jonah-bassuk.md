Jonah Bassuk is a Public Policy student at Cornell's Brooks School with a minor in Business. On campus
he serves on Cornell's Brooks School Dean's Student Advisory Council and the university's Student
Activities Funding Commission.

Beyond Cornell, Jonah is the founder and CEO of Strong Heroes, a nonprofit dedicated to advancing
health policy, research, and community engagement. He previously worked as a private equity summer
analyst at Cerberus Capital Management, evaluating investment opportunities across defense technology,
artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, supply chain, and critical infrastructure.
