Karolina Ostrowski is a student at Cornell University studying Public Policy with minors in Artificial
Intelligence and Business. As a D.C. Start Scholar she developed an early focus on AI governance and
technology policy.

She serves as an Artificial Intelligence Research Intern at USC Marshall School of Business, where she
analyzes AI regulatory frameworks across more than 50 countries, and as a Technology Operations Intern
at Blue Voice. On campus she is an Analyst for the Cornell Political Strategy Group and a member of Phi
Lambda Sigma. Her work sits at the intersection of AI governance, regulatory strategy, and responsible
technology deployment.
