Fiona is a Policy Analysis and Management major in Cornell's Brooks School, with minors in Business,
Information Science, and Law and Society. On campus she is a Laidlaw Scholar, an Emerging Markets
Institute Fellow, a Red Key Student-Athlete Honor Society member, and competes on the Division I
Varsity Fencing Team.

Beyond campus she has conducted research on U.S. defense policy at a Washington, D.C. lobbying firm and
explored peacebuilding strategies at a peace education hub in Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina.
