Esteban Lau studied Government in the College of Arts and Sciences with a minor in International
Relations. On campus he was vice president of Esports at Cornell and a trumpet player for the Big Red
Marching Band.

His main research interests lie in the evolution of warfare, arms procurement, national security, and
foreign policy.
