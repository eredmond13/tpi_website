J. Wesley Hutto is an Associate Professor within the US Air Force's School of Advanced Air and Space
Studies. His research interests include drone warfare and its intersection with International
Relations theory, regional powers and comparative regional security, military strategy, and
multinational military exercises.

His work has appeared in the Journal of Regional Security, International Politics, Defence Studies,
RUSI Journal, and The Army War College Quarterly Journal: Parameters.
