Cynthia Tan is a Public Policy major with a minor in Information Science, concentrating in Information
Ethics, Law, and Policy. On campus she is a Resident Advisor and a member of Phi Alpha Delta Pre-Law
Fraternity, Cornell International Affairs Society, Women Leaders of Color, and a Laidlaw Scholar.

She has served as a Global Data Consultant for the International Union for Conservation of Nature and
the United Nations Development Programme, and as a researcher at the Kaschak Institute for Social
Justice. She was a delegate at COP28 and has collaborated with UN Women. Her current research as a
Laidlaw Scholar focuses on the international legal and policy implications of economic cybercrime.
