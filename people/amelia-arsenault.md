Amelia C. Arsenault is a PhD candidate at Cornell University's Department of Government, in the
international relations subfield. Her research explores the implications of emerging technology on
international relations, with a particular focus on AI, surveillance, and smart city technologies.

She contributed a chapter to the forthcoming *Oxford Handbook of AI Governance*, and her work has been
published in the Canadian Foreign Policy Journal, the Harvard Business Review, and the Journal of
Peace Research. Her doctoral research has received funding from the Social Sciences and Humanities
Research Council and the Department of National Defence MINDS Scholarship Initiative.
