Patrick Whitty is the Founder of 5/13, a strategic advisory firm, and a Major in the U.S. Air Force
Reserve. His career has spanned roles in special operations, the intelligence community, space
operations, and advanced technologies.

His work has centered on advancing commercial space adoption, strengthening supply chain resilience in
critical technologies including rare earth minerals, semiconductors, space-enabling infrastructure, and
advanced sensing, and bridging gaps between government, industry, and academia with an emphasis on
venture capital and innovation integration.
