Tami Omole is a Public Policy student in the Brooks School of Public Policy, with a minor in Aerospace Engineering. On campus, she is a member of Cornell University Sustainable Design, where she works on transportation systems and infrastructure policy. She is also a staff writer for The Cornell Daily Sun and serves as Corporate Secretary of the Cornell Media Guild. Beyond Cornell, Tami has worked as a research assistant at the USC Space Engineering Research Center, contributing to the mechanical design and analysis of space systems. Her research interests lie at the intersection of space policy, technology, and national security, with a particular focus on space security and governance, satellite cybersecurity, and the implications of emerging technologies for critical infrastructure.
