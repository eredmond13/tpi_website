Matthew Jordan is a student at Cornell University studying Public Policy, minoring in International Relations and Public Policy. He developed an interest in federal foreign policy and trade policy, leading him to go to Washington D.C. in his Freshman year fall semester as part of the D.C. start program. He currently serves as a scholar within the Institute of Politics and Global Affairs. On campus, he advocates for state policies benefiting the student body through the Office of Student Government Relations and champions a transformative and human-centered foreign policy through the Cornell Diplomat and the Cornell International Affairs Society. He focuses on the intersection of foreign policy, economics and climate change, particularly through his focus on the Arctic region and economy.
