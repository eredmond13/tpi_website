Luke Flores is a Tech Policy Institute junior fellow and student at Ithaca High School, with technical
interests in engineering systems, cybersecurity, and applied research at the intersection of hardware
and computation. He is currently a research assistant in Cornell University's Mechanical and Aerospace
Engineering Department, where he works on semiconductor supply-chain security and cybersecurity risks
to critical infrastructure.

Luke has participated in competitive STEM and entrepreneurship programs, including the North Carolina
School of Science and Mathematics Summer Accelerator and the Appalachia Entrepreneurship Academy. His
academic work spans statistics, physics, computer science, and history. Outside the lab he is active in
varsity tennis and chess club leadership, and teaches bilingual STEM education programs.
