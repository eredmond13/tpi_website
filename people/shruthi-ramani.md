Shruthi Ramani is an M.S. student in Data Science for Public Policy at Cornell University. Her
research interests sit at the intersection of artificial intelligence, public policy, and digital
governance, with a focus on how data-driven technologies can support effective, equitable, and
evidence-based policymaking.

Her work draws on experience in education policy, behavioral decision science, and comparative
technology regulation, including research on digital dark patterns and AI governance challenges across
national contexts. She is especially interested in AI governance and defense, geopolitics and
technology, and drones and robotics.
