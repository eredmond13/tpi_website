Vance is a sophomore majoring in Government with a minor in Computer Science in the College of Arts & Sciences. On campus, he is a member of the Cornell University Parole Initiative and a Fellow of the pre-government society Pi Lambda Sigma. He previously served as a Defense Policy Analyst with Defense Tech for Ukraine, where he conducted research in drone warfare development. He is a current intern at the Serpens Center for International Security, a Ukrainian defense think tank.
