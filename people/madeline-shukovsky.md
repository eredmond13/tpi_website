Maddie is a freshman majoring in Public Policy at the Jeb E. Brooks School of Public Policy. On campus
she is an analyst for Cornell Policy Group in the foreign center and a consultant for Applied Public
Policy Strategies group.

As a Junior Fellow for the Tech Policy Institute she conducts research on space security. Maddie loves
to learn languages and is interested in the intersection of national security, international relations,
and technology policy.
