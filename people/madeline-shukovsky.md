Maddie is a sophomore majoring in Public Policy at the Jeb E. Brooks School of Public Policy. On campus, Maddie is a foreign analyst for Cornell Policy Group and a consultant on AI upskilling for Applied Public Policy Strategies group. She also works as a data analyst for the Tenants Advocacy Program, informing program resource allocation and tenant support strategies. As a Junior Fellow for the Tech Policy Institute, she conducts research on the semiconductor supply chain, turning technical findings into lay terminology for congressional audiences. Maddie loves to learn languages and is interested in the intersection of national security, international relations, and technology policy.
