Anna Blechová's research focuses on cyber and space law, with particular emphasis on the New Space
sector. She is also involved in an EU-funded project on novel lawful interception tools and the
digitalization of justice.

She holds a law degree from Masaryk University and has interned at the Law Branch of the NATO
Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence and at the National Cyber and Information Security
Agency. She regularly collaborates with the NATO CCDCOE on the Locked Shields exercise and has
presented at conferences including CyCon in Tallinn and the International Astronautical Congress in
Sydney.

Alongside her doctoral studies she has worked as a cybersecurity manager at the Prosecutor General's
Office in Czechia and at the Masaryk University Ombuds Office. She was a visiting researcher at the
Leiden University Institute of Air and Space Law, and is currently a Fulbright Scholar at George
Washington University and Cornell University.
