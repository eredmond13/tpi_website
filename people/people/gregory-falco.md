Gregory Falco is an Assistant Professor at the Sibley School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering
and the Systems Engineering Program at Cornell University. He received his PhD from MIT, where NASA's
Jet Propulsion Laboratory funded his doctoral research in cybersecurity at MIT's Computer Science and
Artificial Intelligence Laboratory.

He is the director of the Aerospace ADVERSARY Laboratory, which designs and develops next-generation
autonomous, secure, and resilient space infrastructure. His space technology research is
policy-relevant and advances standards and national security conversations relating to space
supremacy and cybersecurity.

He was listed in Forbes 30 Under 30 for Enterprise Technology, named a DARPA RISER, and received the
DARPA Young Faculty Award.
