Meleah Grover is an administrative and event coordination professional at the Jeb E. Brooks
School of Public Policy. She supports the Center on Democracy by bringing together scholars,
policymakers, journalists, and global leaders to address critical issues in democracy,
governance, and public policy worldwide.

In her role, Meleah manages event logistics, speaker visits, travel coordination, and key
partnerships that connect Cornell faculty and students with vital global conversations. Her work
directly elevates core programming, including Connecting Cornell to Congress, the Global
Democracy Defenders initiatives, and the non-resident fellows program.
