Jean-François Bélanger is an expert on nuclear politics, disruptive and emerging technologies, and
coercive diplomacy and conflict escalation.

He is currently leading Wargaming as Experiments: Strategy and Unintended Consequences, funded by the
European Union's Horizon 2020 fund as part of his Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellowship. The project uses
geopolitical simulations to better understand the causes of nuclear escalation and restraint.

Jean-François is interested in the ways geopolitical simulations can generate data on rare events,
develop better heuristics for crisis decision-making, and inform military exercises. He was
previously a fellow at Yale's International Security Studies and at the Balsillie School of
International Affairs.
