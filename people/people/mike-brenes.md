Michael Brenes is Senior Lecturer in Global Affairs at Yale University and a Senior Non-Resident
Fellow at the Center for International Policy.

He is the author of *For Might and Right: Cold War Defense Spending and the Remaking of American
Democracy* and *The Rivalry Peril: How Great-Power Competition Threatens Peace and Weakens Democracy*,
co-authored with Van Jackson. He is also co-editor, with Daniel Bessner, of *Rethinking U.S. Power*
and *Cold War Liberalism: Power in a Time of Emergency*.

His work has been published in The New York Times, Foreign Affairs, Foreign Policy, Politico, The New
Republic, Dissent, Boston Review, The Nation, and The Chronicle of Higher Education. He is currently
writing a history of the War on Terror from the 1990s to the present.
