Professor Kennedy-Pipe is regarded as one of the UK's leading scholars of war. In her work with the
UK Parliament, NATO, and the United Nations, she has advised on new and emerging technologies in
modern warfare, from IEDs to drones, and the changing landscape of geopolitics in the High North and
Arctic.

A past Chair and President of the British International Studies Association, Caroline was the 2024
recipient of the organization's Distinguished Contribution Prize, and in 2025 she was elected a
Fellow of the Academy of Social Sciences. She is currently Professor of War Studies at the University
of Loughborough and a specialist advisor to the House of Commons Select Committee on Defence.
