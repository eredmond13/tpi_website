David Silbey is the Director of Teaching and Learning at the Cornell in Washington program in the
Jeb E. Brooks School of Public Policy. He specializes in the industrialized total wars of the 20th
century and the asymmetric responses, including guerrilla warfare, insurgency, and terrorism.

He has written books on the British Army in World War I, the Philippine-American War, the Boxer
Rebellion in China, and US military encounters with non-western nations. He is the series editor for
Cornell University Press' military history series "Battlegrounds: Cornell Studies in Military
History." He received his Ph.D. at Duke University and his BA at Cornell University.
