Ronak Majee is a Junior Fellow at the Critical Infrastructure Hub at the Cornell Tech Policy Institute. He is a sophomore in Cornell's ILR School with intended minors in Economics, Statistics, and China & Asia-Pacific Studies. On campus, he is involved with the Cornell Emerging Markets Institute Club and the Cornell Political Union. This past summer, he interned in the district office for Ohio's 7th Congressional District. His interests include industrial and technology policy, sovereign finance, and the political and economic history of Asia and the Middle East and North Africa.
