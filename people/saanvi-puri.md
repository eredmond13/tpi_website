Saanvi Puri is a Public Policy student in the Brooks School, with minors in Information Science and Philosophy. On campus, she is a volunteer for the Veterans Law Practicum, holds a position in the Cornell Undergraduate Law and Society Review, and is a member of both the Cornell Undergraduate Parole Initiative and the Undergraduate International Law Review. Off campus, she is a Research Assistant at Columbia Law School’s Human Rights Institute where she contributes on the legal end to the development of legal machine learning software, and writes research briefs on human rights concerns internationally. Saanvi hopes to learn more about the intersection between tech policy and civil liberty, and the underlying tech infrastructure that promotes human rights values.
