Kashyap Rajesh is a student at Cornell University studying Information Science and Political Science
with a minor in AI. For the past five years he has led AI policy at Encode, the world's first and
largest youth-powered movement for safe and responsible AI, where he built a global chapter network,
developed literacy programs reaching nearly 50,000 students, and advocated for state-level AI
legislation.

He serves as a Youth Fellow for the Rithm Project and researches AI-induced job disruption at the JEM
Lab for Generative AI at Work. He founded and serves as President of the Cornell Political Strategy
Group and directs the Environmental Technology Center at the Cornell Policy Group.
