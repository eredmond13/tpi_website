William Rosenthal is a Computer Science student at Cornell University, minoring in Policy Analysis and Management. In 2026, he took a temporary leave from Cornell to work as a Cyber Security Assistant at NATO's Cyber Security Centre in The Hague, focusing on cyber resilience and emerging technologies. Previously, he conducted research at Cornell's Aerospace ADVERSARY Lab and co-authored an IEEE-published paper on resilient satellite networks and submarine communications cable failover. He also co-founded Samaritan Scout, a nonprofit platform that connects volunteers with local opportunities, and he previously served as President of the Cornell Cybersecurity Club (now Cyber@Cornell). His interests converge on cybersecurity, technological innovation, space security, and national security policy.
