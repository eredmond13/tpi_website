Paul Kurgan is a senior in the College of Arts & Sciences double majoring in Government and Philosophy. He served as a Political Studies Fellow at the Hertog Foundation and spent a year abroad at Oxford reading Politics and Philosophy, where he earned first-class marks. With funding from the Brettschneider Fund, he studied how artificial intelligence becomes politicized in the context of local elections, interviewing voters, politicians, and AI experts across the Paris and Washington, DC mayoral races. He is currently analyzing that data for his senior Government honors thesis. His interests lie in the history of the American founding and political philosophy, and in how law and policy can keep the human at the center of both the university and our politics.
