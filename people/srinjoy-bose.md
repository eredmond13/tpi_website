Srinjoy Bose is Associate Professor of Politics and International Relations at the University of New
South Wales in Australia. His research focuses on Critical Peace and Security Studies, including the
global governance of military technologies.

He is Associate Editor of the Australian Journal of International Affairs and co-Editor of *Drones
and Global Order: The Implications of Remote Warfare for International Society*. His research on the
racialization and global governance of drone warfare has informed the UN's work on Human Rights in
Africa and the UN's High-Level Advisory Body on AI.
