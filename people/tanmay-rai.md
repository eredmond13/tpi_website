Tanmay Rai is a Government student in the College of Arts & Sciences at Cornell University. On campus, he competes on Cornell’s debate team and is a member of the Alexander Hamilton Society. As a Junior Fellow at the Tech Policy Institute, he researches the intersection of national security and critical infrastructure. His interests include international relations, military history, law, and the broader strategic implications of emerging technologies.
