Elizabeth H. Redmond is TPI's Lead Research Associate supporting DoD-funded research on
semiconductor supply chain security and cybersecurity assessment. She also advances
NATO-relevant research on Full Spectrum Drone Warfare with Dr. James Patton Rogers and
contributes to TPI's Space Hub.

A Subject Matter Expert at the Homeland Defense Institute at the United States Air Force
Academy, her expertise spans hybrid warfare, gray-zone aggression, supply chain security, and
critical infrastructure resilience, with a focus on how contemporary conflict targets the systems
underpinning civilian and military power. She holds an M.S. in Regional Science and a B.S. in
Urban and Regional Studies with honors from Cornell University.
