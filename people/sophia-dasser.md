Sophia Dasser is a junior in Cornell's College of Arts & Sciences, double majoring in Computer Science and Philosophy with minors in Artificial Intelligence and Law and Society. A Milstein Scholar in Technology and Humanity, Laidlaw Leadership and Research Scholar, Telluride Scholar and Beck Entrepreneurship Fellow, she serves as Editor in Chief of The Cornell Daily Sun, leading a staff of 200 and authoring the paper's first bylaws and inaugural AI policy. At TPI, under Professor Sarah Kreps, she contributed to the analysis of Anthropic's federal defense contracts alongside the parallel shifts in its consumer terms of service. She researches uncertainty quantification for large language models with Professor Jed Stiglitz at Cornell Law School, testing whether model confidence holds up in high-stakes legal settings, and leads data visualization for the Cañizares Center for Emerging Markets' annual report. Her earlier work spans remote sensing and machine learning on agricultural abandonment in Morocco's High Atlas and federal appropriations advocacy with UNICEF USA, where she represented the United States at the United Nations General Assembly. She has been featured in The New York Times for her advocacy, and her interests center on AI evaluation, procurement and governance.
