Adi Rao is a PhD candidate at Cornell University's Department of Government and an associate at the
RAND Corporation.

He researches national security and technology issues, including space politics, cyber defense, and
the political economy of semiconductor chip supply chains. He earned his BA in Economics from NYU in
2015 and an MA in Politics from Columbia University in 2018.
