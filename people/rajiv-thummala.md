Rajiv Thummala is pursuing a PhD in Aerospace Engineering at Cornell University with a minor in
International Relations. He graduated summa cum laude and as a NASA Space Grant Scholar from
Pennsylvania State University in 2023, where he earned his M.S. and B.S. degrees in Cybersecurity.

As a graduate researcher at the Aerospace ADVERSARY Laboratory, Rajiv is developing disruptive space
technology. His research interests are in aerospace security, space combat power projection, and
national security space topics.
