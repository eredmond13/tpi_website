Kayla Schechter is a Public Policy student in the Brooks School of Public Policy. On campus she is a
speaker curator for the TEDx conference team, a Democracy Fellow with the Cornell Center on Global
Democracy, and a member of the Cornell Intellectual Property and Ethics Club.

Inspired by studying abroad in Jerusalem and interning for the French Consulate as well as local
government, Kayla is interested in the intersection between international relations, technology policy,
and national security.
