LTC Paul Lushenko is the Director of Special Operations and a Faculty Instructor in the U.S. Army War
College's Department of Military Strategy, Planning, and Operations.

He earned his Ph.D. and M.A. in International Relations from Cornell University. He also holds an
M.A. in Defense and Strategic Studies from the U.S. Naval War College, an M.A. in International
Relations and a Master of Diplomacy from The Australian National University, and a B.S. from the U.S.
Military Academy.

He is the co-editor of *Drones and Global Order: Implications of Remote Warfare for International
Society*, the first book to systematically study the implications of drone warfare on global
politics. He also has a book forthcoming entitled *The Legitimacy of Drone Warfare: Evaluating Public
Perceptions*.
