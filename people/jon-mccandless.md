Jon McCandless is an Ignite Postdoctoral Fellow at Cornell University, where he is working to
commercialize his research on semiconductors.

Prior to Cornell, Jon worked at the Air Force Research Laboratory focusing on device fabrication and
testing of gallium nitride and gallium oxide devices. He was a member of the ACCESS Center, a joint
center between AFRL and Cornell devoted to gallium oxide. He earned his PhD in Electrical and
Computer Engineering at Cornell, received the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship, and was an Irwin
Jacobs Family Fellow.
