Thej Khanna is a senior studying English Literature at Cornell University's College of Arts and
Sciences. On campus he is a Student Researcher with the Digital Due Process Clinic and conducts
research with Senior Fellow Judith Germano on enterprise cybersecurity risks associated with autonomous
and agentic decision-making systems.

He writes for the Cornell Policy Review and previously served as Co-President of the Cornell American
Civil Liberties Union. Thej is currently a Product Management Intern at Ocient, working on agentic AI
governance and data privacy. He has previously worked in the Appeals Bureau of the Bronx District
Attorney's Office and with the Surveillance Technology Oversight Project.
