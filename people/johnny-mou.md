Johnny Mou is a sophomore majoring in Industrial and Labor Relations who transferred from the
University of Illinois Urbana Champaign, where he was a Computer Science and Economics major.

On campus he interns for the Ultimate Reentry Opportunity initiative, an organization dedicated to
supporting the reintegration of formerly incarcerated individuals. He is a brother of Phi Alpha Delta
and serves as a Staff Writer for the Undergraduate International Law Review. His research interests
focus on the intersection of artificial intelligence, national security, and the law.
