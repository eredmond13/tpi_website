Nicholas Varela studied Computer Science and Government in the College of Arts and Sciences, focused
on cloud, emerging, and government technologies. He spent part of his 2023-2024 academic year at the
University of Edinburgh studying UK Politics and Political Data Analysis, and was part of the Summer
2022 cohort of Cornell in Washington.

At Cornell he served as President of Perfect Match, a machine learning incubator for connecting
students, and was an Arts and Sciences Ambassador and Teaching Assistant for the Computer Science
Department. He interned with Amazon for three summers in their US Government Tech Sector and joined
Amazon full-time upon graduation in 2025.
