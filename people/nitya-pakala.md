Nitya Pakala is a student majoring in Information Science with a concentration in Data Science and a
minor in Artificial Intelligence. On campus she leads the product team at Digital Tech and Innovation
and is active in Design Consulting at Cornell and Social Enterprise Group at Cornell.

Her work spans AI product strategy and responsible technology deployment across projects in logistics,
data, and emerging markets, extending into policy recommendations on gender equity and digital access
as a UN Women youth representative. Her research interests center on human-centered AI design,
algorithmic governance, and how platform dynamics, geopolitical forces, and compute infrastructure
shape global technology ecosystems.
