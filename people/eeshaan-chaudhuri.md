Eeshaan Chaudhuri is a Public Policy student at Cornell's Brooks School, with minors in International
Relations, Data Science, and Philosophy. On campus he enjoys walking backward while giving tours,
writing for the Cornell Policy Review, and representing his peers in Cornell's Student and University
Assemblies.

Eeshaan has contributed to projects with the New Jersey State Legislature, the U.S. Congress, NATO, and
the Department of Defense. His research explores human behavior, grand strategy, and the role of
emerging public interest technologies in governance.
