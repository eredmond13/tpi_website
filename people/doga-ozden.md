Doğa Özden is a third-year J.D. student at Cornell Law School. His research interests span AI
governance, constitutional law, and technology policy. His current research explores the
constitutional dimensions of AI governance, including First Amendment limits on government-directed
modifications to AI systems.

Doğa is the creator of PropBench, an open-source benchmark for evaluating large language model
reasoning through propositional logic proof efficiency. A Myron Taylor Scholar and Berger Fellow, he
holds an MA in Philosophy from York University. Following graduation he will join Latham & Watkins'
Silicon Valley office, focusing on tech transactions and emerging companies.
