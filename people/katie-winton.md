Katie Winton is an undergraduate in Cornell's College of Arts and Sciences studying Computer Science
with a minor in Policy Analysis and Management. Her research with the Tech Policy Institute and
Cornell's Aerospace Adversary Lab focuses on semiconductor supply-chain security.

On campus she is a software developer for the Engineers for a Sustainable World project team and a
Staff Writer for the Arts and Culture section of The Cornell Daily Sun. Her other interests include
mathematics, reading, and art.
