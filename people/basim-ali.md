Basim has been a public servant for the Government of India with expertise in technological and
fintech policy analysis, risk advisory, and strategy consulting. He is currently pursuing his MPA from
the Cornell Jeb E. Brooks School of Public Policy.

In India, at the federal level, he was instrumental in formulating and executing policies dealing with
cryptocurrency regulations, Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Financing of Terrorism, along with
regulating peer-to-peer lending platforms. At Deloitte, he was part of a global team handling
financial crime investigations, anti-corruption due diligence, and business intelligence. In his spare
time he enjoys hiking and reading poetry.
