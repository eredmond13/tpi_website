Avishai Melamed is a PhD student at Cornell University's Department of Government in the
international relations subfield. His research focuses on the politics of technology, science
diplomacy, and patterns of international cooperation and competition.

He has published in Acta Astronautica and the Journal of Space Safety Engineering, and written for the
Center for Space Policy and Strategy and Brookings TechStream. Avishai holds a Bachelor of Arts in
Political Science and History from the University of California, San Diego.
