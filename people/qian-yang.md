Qian Yang is a human-AI interaction designer and Cornell's assistant professor in information
science. Her research focuses on understanding how to bring human-centered thinking to bear on
frontier AI techniques more effectively.

Her recent work focuses on leveraging large language models for education and mental healthcare, and
in doing so promoting the new practice of designing AI technologies and policies simultaneously.
