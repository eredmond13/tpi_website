On October 7, 2023, Hamas used drones to initiate its attack on Israel. On June 1, 2025, Ukraine
conducted Operation Spider Web, using over 100 drones to strike Russian military bases and destroy a
third of the country's strategic bombers. Later that month Israel used drones to destroy Iran's air
defences ahead of a strike on Iranian missile sites.

A new brief by Senior Fellow Dr. Paul Lushenko and Joseph Amoroso argues that these trends leave the
U.S. military needing to defend against drones everywhere and all the time.

Proliferation now crosses the boundary between states and non-state actors, with the Islamic State
having developed a bureaucratic drone programme of its own. The threat is domestic too. In December
2023, dozens of drones disrupted operations at Joint Base Langley-Eustis in Virginia, forcing the
relocation of the F-22 Raptors responsible for defending the homeland.
