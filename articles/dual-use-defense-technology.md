There is a line somewhere between a commercial drone and an unmanned combat aerial vehicle, between a
data analytics platform and a targeting system. This op-ed asks not whether that line exists but who
drew it, how it evolved, and who benefits from keeping it where it is.

Companies are encouraged to classify their own products, regulatory authority is split between two
agencies with conflicting mandates, and the main international regime runs on consensus any single
member can veto. The result is an accountability problem rather than a technical one, and one that
artificial intelligence makes considerably harder.
