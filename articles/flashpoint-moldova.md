This brief examines how the erosion of the international order could constrain America's response to a Russian-backed crisis in Moldova. It draws on a bipartisan tabletop exercise with senior political and defense leaders, regional experts and journalists.

Weakened alliances left participants with fewer options, and cautious reassurance of Romania invited further Russian pressure. More independent allies also raised the risk of escalation Washington could not control. The brief closes by arguing that trust and relationships must be built before a crisis begins.
