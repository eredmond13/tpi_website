In September, Professor Ronald J. Deibert delivered a lecture at the institute on the ethical
implications and real-world impacts of Citizen Lab's research into mercenary surveillance and digital
transnational repression.

Deibert set out the growing risks posed by invasive surveillance technologies, and the part they play
in undermining privacy, human rights, and democracy worldwide.
