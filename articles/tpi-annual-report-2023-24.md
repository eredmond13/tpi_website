The 2023-24 report documents a year of record growth, when the team expanded 65 percent from 20
fellows to 33 and competitive research funding reached $1.3 million across six grants.

Alongside seventeen events and fifteen distinguished speakers, the institute delivered 73 policy and
press briefings and twelve monthly research reports, and introduced two new academic courses within
the Brooks School.

The report sets out the year's funded projects, including the $1M Bitcoin and financial freedom study
and the NATO Science for Peace and Security drone programme, and profiles the fellows across all
tiers.
