This is a **placeholder note**. Notes are the shorter form in Signals, usually
400 to 600 words, and are what most fellows write. Delete this file, or write
over it, whenever you like.

A note has no `format` line, which is the only difference between it and a
column. It appears in the same list, with a small square photograph and a
compact headline, so a reader can tell at a glance which pieces are which.

Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. **Bold** uses two asterisks either
side, and a link looks like [this](https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/btpi/).

To put a piece on a hub page, add a line such as `hubs: ["drones"]` at the top.
Both example pieces leave it off, so they appear only here.
