On March 8th our founder and director, Professor Sarah Kreps, was profiled by TechCrunch as part of
its Women in AI series. The article covers her journey and her contributions to the field, especially
as they bear on U.S. foreign and defence policy.

A U.S. Air Force veteran, Professor Kreps works on the political implications of AI technologies such
as OpenAI's GPT models. She examines the potential for AI to generate credible disinformation,
running experiments to assess its impact on democracy and on legislative processes.

The piece also covers the challenges facing women entering the AI field and her advice to them,
alongside her views on responsible AI development and the ethical questions that come with it.
