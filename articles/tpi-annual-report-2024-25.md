The 2024-25 report documents twelve months of consolidation and growth, and is the most current
single account of the institute's work.

The team grew 27 percent over the year, from 33 fellows to 42, made up of two in leadership, seventeen
senior fellows, fifteen fellows, and ten junior fellows. Competitive research funding reached $4.5
million, an increase of 246 percent, across nine grants. The institute hosted 21 events with ten
distinguished speakers, delivered 175 policy and press briefings, and published twelve monthly
research reports.

The report also sets out the five research hubs, the year's grant and gift awarding institutions,
research and teaching activity, and profiles of every fellow.
