On September 15, 2025, the institute hosted Dr. Jean-François Bélanger of the Royal Danish Defence
College for a discussion of NATO and Europe's defensive capabilities.

The conversation set out why Europe needs predictable, long-term industrial and procurement plans,
and why doctrine, platforms, and logistics have to align with one another as new capabilities come
into service.

Dr. Bélanger's work, both in *A Blueprint for a European Defense Force* and in *Virtual Backgrounds*
(forthcoming, Bloomsbury), argues for a pragmatic and phased approach.

We were fortunate to hear from one of our own Non-Resident Fellows. At the Royal Danish Defence
College he is an Assistant Professor, teaching and researching European security, military strategy,
and defence cooperation.
