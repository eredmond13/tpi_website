---
title: Tech Policy Institute launches two-year study on financial privacy
date: 2026-02-06
category: News
summary: A collaboration with the Bitcoin Policy Institute and Fedi, examining how public policy and enforcement shape behaviour around privacy technologies.
---

The Bitcoin Policy Institute, the Cornell Tech Policy Institute, and Fedi are leading a new
two-year integrated study on American attitudes toward financial privacy. The project is the first
to systematically examine how public policy and enforcement shape both user behaviour and
developer decisions around privacy technologies.

As Director Sarah Kreps notes, the study will move debates about privacy from the abstract to the
evidential, tracking how policy signals affect public behaviour and developer choices over time.

The research combines nationally representative surveys of U.S. users with in-depth interviews of
developers working on Bitcoin and related open-source systems, with four semi-annual reports
beginning in April 2026 and running through 2027.
