summary: A roundtable in the Eisenhower Series on the value of alliances in a new era of geopolitics, with four officers from the US Army War College.

The institute hosted officers from the US Army War College as part of the Eisenhower Series. The
series was established to encourage civil-military discussion during the Vietnam War, and today
serves as a forum between active service military and universities.

Joining us for a roundtable and open discussion were Colonel Paul Rob Haertsch, an Australian Army
officer and former Defence Advisor to Fiji; Lieutenant Colonel Christiana Crawford, a U.S. Marine
Corps Reserve operational planner and Western Europe Foreign Area Officer; Lieutenant Colonel Frank
Hartnett, a Public Affairs Officer in the U.S. Air Force and former Public Affairs Advisor to the
Secretary of the Air Force; and Colonel Leon J. Perkowski '93, Assistant Professor in the Department
of National Security and Strategy at the U.S. Army War College and Director of the Eisenhower Series
College Program.

Chaired by Executive Director Dr James Patton Rogers, the roundtable focused on the value of
alliances in a new era of geopolitics. The speakers described nonlinear career paths held together
by a shared appetite for learning, drawing on experience that ranged from pursuing several degrees
to working with defence attachés and at the highest levels of defence politics.
