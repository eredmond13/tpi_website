On Thursday May 1st the institute welcomed Dr Anatoly Levshin of Harvard University for two events
discussing his research on artificial intelligence and geopolitics.

Levshin set out how AI is likely to catalyse systemic transformations, singling out oracular
foresight and virtuosic creativity as the features states will reach for in advancing their
interests.

He also examined the prospect of abductive AI shaping the future battlefield, at the level of command
as well as at the operational level.
