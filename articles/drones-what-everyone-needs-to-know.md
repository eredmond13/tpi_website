Director Sarah Kreps and Executive Director James Patton Rogers have published their latest book,
*Drones: What Everyone Needs to Know*, with Oxford University Press.

It offers a comprehensive and updated look at the rapidly evolving world of drones, covering the
past, present, and future of military and civilian applications, and how drones have transformed
industries and warfare.
