Research often assumes that junior military personnel will trust AI in future wars, and trust it more
readily than senior officers do.

In this article, published in *Armed Forces & Society*, Non-Resident Senior Fellow Dr. Paul Lushenko
tests the claim that cadets are digital natives who bring high levels of trust to partnering with AI.
The study uses an original survey experiment among a representative sample of cadets assigned to the
Reserve Officers' Training Corps programme in the United States.

Cadets do turn out to be more trusting of AI-enhanced military technologies than senior officers.
Their trust, though, is shaped by a more conservative understanding of the appropriate use and
oversight of AI than the digital-native framing would predict.

The article provides the first experimental evidence of cadets' trust in AI, with implications for
future research, for policy, and for military modernisation.
