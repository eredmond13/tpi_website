Before the Tech Policy Institute, there was the Cornell Tech Policy Lab, directed by Sarah Kreps and
described as a community of faculty, researchers, and students investigating the intersection between
technology and politics.

This report sets out the lab's leadership team, faculty associates, senior fellows, graduate fellows,
and undergraduate research associates, alongside its mission and administrative structure.

It covers research on how emerging technologies shape the use of force abroad, the implications of
online misinformation for electoral integrity, and the effects of social media and cryptocurrencies
on democratic accountability, with a full list of media coverage, invited seminars, and peer-reviewed
publications.
