On April 11th the institute welcomed Dr. Jonathan Stringfield, Vice President of Global Business
Research and Marketing at Activision Blizzard.

His presentation gave a background to the gaming industry and described its close ties to emerging
technologies such as virtual reality and artificial intelligence.

Attendees heard from an accomplished expert in a corner of the technology sector that is less often
discussed, and came away with a better sense of how digital worlds can both model and affect
real-world policy.
