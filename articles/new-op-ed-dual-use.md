There is a line somewhere between a commercial drone and an unmanned combat aerial vehicle, between
a data analytics platform and a targeting system, between satellite internet and military
communications infrastructure.

In a new op-ed, Junior Fellow Sumajja Denysiuk asks not whether that line exists but who drew it, how
it evolved, and who benefits from keeping it where it is. Companies are encouraged to classify their
own products, regulatory authority is split between two agencies with conflicting mandates, and the
main international regime runs on consensus any single member can veto.

The result, the piece argues, is a system quietly captured by private actors with strong financial
incentives to minimise oversight.

The op-ed is part of a series written by TPI Junior Fellows.
