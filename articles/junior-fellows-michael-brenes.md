On October 2, the institute hosted a wide-ranging conversation with Professor Michael Brenes,
Co-Director of the Brady-Johnson Program in Grand Strategy and Lecturer in History at Yale
University, alongside Professor Michael John Williams, Associate Professor of Public Administration
and International Affairs at the Maxwell School at Syracuse University.

Fellows gathered to hear from Professor Brenes about the ideas behind his book *The Rivalry Peril:
How Great-Power Competition Threatens Peace and Weakens Democracy*. The discussion covered the
resurgence of great-power competition, the strategic posture the United States must adopt in a
shifting global landscape, and the democratic, industrial, and policy trade-offs embedded in
American defense strategy.

The exchange was thought-provoking and challenging, offering fresh insight into how power, policy,
and global dynamics intersect today.

Our thanks go to Professors Brenes and Williams for their time and expertise, and to our fellows for
the thoughtful questions they brought to the discussion.
