On April 17th the institute welcomed Michael Daniel for a talk on careers in tech policy.

Michael served as Special Assistant to the President and Cybersecurity Coordinator on the National
Security Council staff under President Obama, and is President and CEO of the Cyber Threat Alliance.

Attendees heard from a seasoned professional with a wealth of experience, and came away with a
clearer sense of how to make a tangible impact in the field.
