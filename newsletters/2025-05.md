      - title: An attack on America's universities is an attack on American power
        text: >-
          Sarah Kreps wrote an article for Foreign Affairs on the dangers of the Trump
          administration's freezing of federal grants to universities. She traced how cooperation
          between institutions shaped research in the Second World War and the Cold War, producing
          world-changing inventions including the internet, and warned that severing the connection
          between universities and the Department of Defense may push talent to other countries or
          the private sector, harming longer-term US national security interests.
        link: https://www.foreignaffairs.com/united-states/attack-americas-universities-attack-american-power
        linkLabel: Read the article

      - title: Racial bias and public support for US drone strikes
        text: >-
          Paul Lushenko co-authored a paper for Security Studies using two novel image-based survey
          experiments. It finds that public attitudes toward US drone strikes are not shaped by the
          targets' skin colour, that Americans with racist worldviews are more likely to support
          drone strikes regardless of location or target, and that the considerations Americans
          weigh are not reducible to race.
        link: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/09636412.2025.2497968
        linkLabel: Read the paper
