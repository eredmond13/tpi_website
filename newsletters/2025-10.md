          leadership and fellows. It was a valuable occasion to engage with the Cornell

      - title: Craig Newmark Philanthropies supports the TPI Cyber Test Range
        text: >-
          Craig Newmark Philanthropies has awarded the institute $140,000 to support the Cyber
          Test Range, designed to detect hidden cyber vulnerabilities in critical hardware.
          Executive Director James Patton Rogers said Craig's passion for cyber civil defence
          continues to inspire the institute's work in cyber tool development and pioneering
          security research.
        link: https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/news/newmark-philanthropies-supports-btpi-cyber-test-range/

        meta: Book release
        text: >-
          Founder and Director Sarah Kreps and Executive Director James Patton Rogers published
          their latest book with Oxford University Press, a comprehensive and updated look at the
          rapidly evolving world of drones. It covers the past, present, and future of military
          and civilian applications, and how drones have transformed industries and warfare.
        link: https://global.oup.com/ushe/product/drones-9780197795644?cc=us&lang=en
