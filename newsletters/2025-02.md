      - title: Lauryn Williams joins as Non-Resident Senior Fellow
        text: >-
          Lauryn was previously Chief of Staff in the Office of the Assistant Secretary of Defense
          for Industrial Base Policy, where she spearheaded release of the first-ever National
          Defense Industrial Strategy Implementation Plan. Immediately prior she was Director for
          Strategy in the Office of the National Cyber Director, driving the White House initiative
          on space system cybersecurity. That work resulted in the first-ever minimum cybersecurity
          requirements for federal space systems, included in President Biden's January 2025
          cybersecurity executive order.

      - title: A $3 million Department of Defense grant for the semiconductor project
        text: >-
          In December the Department of Defense awarded the institute a $3 million grant to fund
          the Semiconductor Research Hub, investigating and enhancing semiconductor supply chain
          resilience. In February the hub reached initial operating capacity for its cyber test
          range and began analysing cybersecurity threats and vulnerabilities.
