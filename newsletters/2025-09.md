      - title: The biomining report
        text: >-
          The institute has released a report urging federal action to advance biomining, the use
          of microorganisms to extract metals from ores and waste streams. Metals like cobalt,
          nickel, and lithium are critical for US defence systems, clean energy, and advanced
          electronics, but China controls a majority of refining capacity, creating chokepoints
          that expose national security risks. The report calls for federal investment to turn
          laboratory breakthroughs into industrial deployment and secure domestic mineral
          independence.
        link: /publications.html
        linkLabel: Read the report
