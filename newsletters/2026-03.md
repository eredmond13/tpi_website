      - title: Gudni Th. Johannesson, former Icelandic President, visits Cornell
        text: >-
          Gudni Th. Johannesson, President of Iceland from 2016 to 2024 and now Professor at the
          University of Iceland, visited Cornell as part of the Messenger Lecture series. Fellows
          engaged with him during a Polar Frontlines simulation on Arctic security and hybrid
          threats, written by the institute and run with the Aerospace ADVERSARY Lab and the
          Homeland Defense Institute. Fellows also joined him for a breakfast where he shared
          stories from his time as Head of State and how his background as a historian shaped his
          connection with Icelandic citizens and leaders. He delivered a History of Iceland lecture
          during the visit.
        link: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brooks-tech-policy-institute1_techpolicyinstitute-tpi/
