      - title: "Securing Semiconductor Supply Chains 2025 Research Grant"
        text: >-
          A new grant opportunity of up to $10,000 to support Cornell students and faculty
          conducting research at the intersection of technology, cybersecurity, and public policy.
          We invite proposals from graduate students and faculty working on semiconductors and
          semiconductor supply chains. Potential topics include cybersecurity vulnerabilities in
          global supply chains, semiconductor design and innovation, supply chain security and
          resilience, regulatory and policy challenges, and the geopolitics of semiconductor
          manufacturing and trade. Applications open on April 1st and are accepted on a rolling
          basis.
